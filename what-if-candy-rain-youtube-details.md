## Title

What If the Sky Rained Candy? 🍬 The Sticky Science

## Description

Imagine a candy-colored downpour over the city! 🍬☔ Follow the falling sweets from rooftops to storm drains, and find out why a lot of sugar in waterways could matter.

The candy storm is imaginary; the cloud and stream science is real. Sources: NOAA on [clouds](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/clouds) and [precipitation](https://prod-01-alb-www-noaa.woc.noaa.gov/jetstream/atmosphere/precipitation), and USGS on [dissolved oxygen](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/dissolved-oxygen-and-water).
