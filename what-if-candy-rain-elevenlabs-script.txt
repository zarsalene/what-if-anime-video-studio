VOICE DIRECTION (voice settings / prompt; do not read aloud)
Warm, gentle, conversational and curious. Natural pacing, soft energy, and a little wonder on the opening question. Keep it reassuring and clear. No ominous or exaggerated delivery. Pause briefly between paragraphs.

NARRATION

What if candy fell from the sky? It would look magical at first.

Gummies bounce on roofs. Jelly beans tumble as people rush out to catch them.

Real clouds are tiny water drops or ice. Candy takes magic.

Gravity pulls candy down. Size, shape, and air drag change its speed.

Hard sweets could sting; soft gummies splat. Impact depends on their size and speed.

Rain melts the sugary coating, washing sticky runoff into storm drains.

In streams, microbes use dissolved oxygen to break down sugar. How much matters; dilution matters too.

Magical at first. Sticky for longer. Would you try it?
